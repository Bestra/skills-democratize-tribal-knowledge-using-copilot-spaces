# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation hub. This collection of guides provides a comprehensive overview of how OctoAcme runs cross-functional projects, from initial ideation through successful delivery and continuous improvement.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Our process is designed to deliver small, testable increments while maintaining transparency and alignment across all stakeholders.

Our project management methodology is built on five core principles:
- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in every decision
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments to reduce risk and enable fast feedback
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has designated Project Manager (PM) and Product Manager (PdM) leads
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and metrics
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage open feedback and continuous learning

## Key Workflows

Our project lifecycle follows a structured five-phase approach:

1. **Initiation**: Define problem statements, identify stakeholders, and establish high-level timelines
2. **Planning**: Break down work into shippable increments, identify dependencies, and align on timelines
3. **Execution**: Build, test, review, and iterate with daily standups and weekly sync meetings
4. **Release**: Deploy with proper verification, rollback plans, and stakeholder communication
5. **Retrospective**: Capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements

## Team Roles and Communication

Our teams are structured around three core roles working in close collaboration:

- **Project Managers**: Coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and facilitate cross-team communication
- **Product Managers**: Define what should be built, prioritize the backlog, and measure customer outcomes
- **Developers**: Design, build, test, and deliver software components while participating in planning and reviews

Communication happens through regular cadences including weekly PM-PdM syncs, twice-weekly team standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and milestone-based demos and reviews.

## Quality Assurance Practices

Quality is built into every phase of our process through:

- **Comprehensive testing**: Unit tests, integration tests, and end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows
- **Code review processes**: Small PRs with required approvals and automated CI/CD checks
- **Risk management**: Proactive identification and mitigation of project risks and dependencies
- **Continuous monitoring**: Dashboards tracking key signals like errors, latency, and usage metrics
- **Iterative improvement**: Regular retrospectives converting learnings into actionable improvements
